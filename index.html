Guía Definitiva: Los 12 Tiempos del Inglés

Estructuras, Reglas Clave, Primera y Tercera Persona con Traducción

Tabla 1: Verbo Regular (Work / Worked / Worked)

Tiempo Verbal Regla Clave Primera Persona (I) Traducción (1ª) Tercera Persona (He)
1. Presente Simple Usa do/does para preguntas/negaciones. 3ª persona lleva -s. I work every day.
I don't work every day.
Do you work every day?		 Trabajo todos los días.
No trabajo todos los días.
¿Trabajas todos los días?		 He works every day.
He doesn't work.
Does he work?
2. Pasado Simple Usa did universal. Verbo en pasado solo en afirmativa. I worked yesterday.
I didn't work yesterday.
Did you work yesterday?		 Trabajé ayer.
No trabajé ayer.
¿Trabajaste ayer?		 He worked yesterday.
He didn't work yesterday.
Did he work yesterday?
3. Futuro Simple Usa will para todas las personas sin excepción. I will work tomorrow.
I won't work tomorrow.
Will you work tomorrow?		 Trabajaré mañana.
No trabajaré mañana.
¿Trabajarás mañana?		 He will work tomorrow.
He won't work tomorrow.
Will he work tomorrow?
4. Presente Continuo Verbo be (am/is/are) + verbo principal con -ing. I am working right now.
I'm not working.
Are you working?		 Estoy trabajando ahora.
No estoy trabajando.
¿Estás trabajando?		 He is working right now.
He isn't working.
Is he working?
5. Pasado Continuo Verbo be en pasado (was/were) + verbo con -ing. I was working when you called.
I wasn't working.
Were you working?		 Estaba trabajando.
No estaba.
¿Estabas trabajando?		 He was working.
He wasn't working.
Was he working?
6. Futuro Continuo Futuro will + puente fijo be + verbo con -ing. I will be working tomorrow.
I won't be working.
Will you be working?		 Estaré trabajando mañana.
No estaré.
¿Estarás trabajando?		 He will be working.
He won't be working.
Will he be working?
7. Presente Perfecto Motor have / has + verbo en participio. I have worked before.
I haven't worked.
Have you worked?		 He trabajado antes.
No he trabajado.
¿Has trabajado?		 He has worked before.
He hasn't worked.
Has he worked?
8. Pasado Perfecto Motor had universal + verbo en participio. I had worked before dinner.
I hadn't worked.
Had you worked?		 Había trabajado antes de cenar.
No había.
¿Habías trabajado?		 He had worked before dinner.
He hadn't worked.
Had he worked?
9. Futuro Perfecto Futuro will have + verbo en participio. I will have worked by 8 p.m.
I won't have worked.
Will you have worked?		 Habré trabajado a las 8 p.m.
No habré.
¿Habrás trabajado?		 He will have worked by 8 p.m.
He won't have worked.
Will he have worked?
10. Pres. Perf. Cont. Perfecto (have/has) + puente been + verbo con -ing. I have been working for 2 hrs.
I haven't been working.
Have you been working?		 He estado trabajando 2 hrs.
No he estado.
¿Has estado trabajando?		 He has been working for 2 hrs.
He hasn't been working.
Has he been working?
11. Pas. Perf. Cont. Pasado perfecto (had) + puente been + verbo con -ing. I had been working.
I hadn't been working.
Had you been working?		 Había estado trabajando.
No había estado.
¿Habías estado?		 He had been working.
He hadn't been working.
Had he been working?
12. Fut. Perf. Cont. Futuro perfecto (will have) + puente been + verbo con -ing. I will have been working.
I won't have been working.
Will you have been working?		 Habré estado trabajando.
No habré estado.
¿Habrás estado?		 He will have been working.
He won't have been working.
Will he have been working?
